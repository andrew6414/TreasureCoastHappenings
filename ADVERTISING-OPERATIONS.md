# Advertising operations — Treasure Coast Happenings

## Introductory packages
- Featured Business: $49/month
- Special of the Week: $35/week
- Homepage Sponsor: $99/month

These are proposed launch rates. They are not automatically billed or collected by the app. Confirm availability, deliverables, dates, payment method, cancellation terms, and written approval with the advertiser before accepting payment.

## How to activate a featured listing
1. Agree on the package, price, start/end date, and placement in writing.
2. Collect payment using a method you choose and issue a receipt. Do not mark a listing sponsored until payment and terms are confirmed.
3. Confirm all listing facts and receive written permission for every photo and logo.
4. In `App.js`, find the exact listing object inside the `listings` array and add `sponsored:true` before its closing brace.
5. Commit the change to GitHub. Sponsored listings automatically move to the top of their category and show a **SPONSORED** label in both the listing card and detail page.
6. When the paid period ends, remove `sponsored:true` and commit again.

## Important
- Never label a listing sponsored unless the business has paid and the agreed placement is active.
- Keep basic listings free and do not imply that payment guarantees positive reviews or editorial coverage.
- Confirm the advertiser has rights to any supplied photo/logo and retain written permission.
- These introductory packages are manually administered. The app does not yet process payments, subscriptions, refunds, or renewals.
