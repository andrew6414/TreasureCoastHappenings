# Image credits and usage notes

Treasure Coast Happenings uses a mix of:
- Free-to-use illustrative photographs from Unsplash.
- Area/category photographs from Wikimedia Commons under the licenses listed below.
- A small number of photos found on official business pages or community photo pages.

**Important:** An image being visible on a business website, Google, a review site, or social media does not automatically make it free to reuse. The official-site and community-sourced photos in the app are not all accompanied by written reuse permission. Before a commercial public launch, obtain permission for those exact images or replace them with appropriately licensed images. Do not describe an illustrative image as a photo of the named business, band, or event.

## Official-page and community photos

Some restaurant, venue, and community photos are linked in `App.js` to their original public pages. These are useful visual references, but public availability is not proof of a reuse license. The project should obtain written permission or replace each such image with a licensed alternative before monetized public distribution.

## General rules

- Illustrative photos must be labeled as illustrative and must not imply they show the named business, performer, or event.
- CC BY and CC BY-SA images require attribution and a license link. CC BY-SA also has share-alike requirements for adaptations.
- If a source image is moved or removed, replace the URL and update this register.
- For commercial distribution, keep a record of the exact source and license/permission for every displayed image.
