# Treasure Coast Happenings — Photo Permission Tracker

This file tracks real photographs approved for display in the app and website.

## Approval rules
- Use a photo only after the photographer/copyright owner grants written permission for display in Treasure Coast Happenings, including commercial use if the app is monetized.
- A venue or performer may submit a photo only if they own it or have permission from the photographer to authorize this use.
- Record the original photographer, permission date, permitted use, required credit, and source file.
- Do not copy photos from Google Images, social media, review sites, or business websites merely because they are publicly visible.
- Keep the written permission email or message with project records.
- Until a listing has an approved image, the app should show its photo-needed placeholder.

## Photo permission request template

Subject: Photo request for Treasure Coast Happenings

Hello,

I’m building Treasure Coast Happenings, a local guide featuring restaurants, food specials, live music and events across Florida’s Treasure Coast.

Would you be willing to provide one or two photos of your business, performer or event for us to display in the app and website?

Please send only photos that you own or are authorized to license. By replying with the photo, please confirm that you own the copyright or have authority from the photographer to grant Treasure Coast Happenings permission to display it in the app and website, including promotional and commercial use. Please also tell us the photographer’s preferred credit.

There is no charge to be listed. Thank you for helping us show the real Treasure Coast!
