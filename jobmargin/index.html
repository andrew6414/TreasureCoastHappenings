Straight answers

What to expect

Will this make $5,000 a month?

That is the target, not a promise. At a $29 price, $5,000 gross revenue requires about 173 sales a month before fees, refunds and taxes. We need to validate demand and build traffic before assuming those sales are possible.

How much time will it take?

Building and validating the first product takes focused work. Once checkout, delivery and search traffic are working, the aim is a few hours a week for support, updates and publishing—not zero work.

Do I need to buy software?

Not to start. The site can be hosted free on GitHub Pages. A digital checkout provider can start on a free plan and take a fee from sales.

Who is this for?

Independent operators and small service businesses such as mobile detailers, cleaners, lawn-care providers and pressure washers. The calculator is a general planning aid; each business should adapt its costs and local pricing.
