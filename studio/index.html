Good to know

Questions, answered.

Will I own my website?

Ownership, access and handoff terms will be documented in your project agreement. We aim to give clients clear access to their site and domain accounts.

Do I need to provide the words and pictures?

You can provide your own content and photos. We can help organize and write website copy; photography, licensed stock and extensive content production may be additional services.

Is hosting included?

Hosting depends on the platform and features selected. We will explain any recurring third-party costs before you approve the project.

How long does a website take?

Timing depends on the scope and how quickly content and feedback are provided. We will give you a project schedule before work begins.

Can you guarantee first-page Google rankings or more sales?

No. We build sound website and search foundations, but search rankings and sales depend on many factors outside any web designer's control.
